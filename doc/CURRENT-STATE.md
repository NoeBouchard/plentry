## Works in product

- **Auth:** email/password (Supabase). **Log in** opens the login form. **Don't have an account?** under it opens sign-up (name, email, password). Sign-up still offers **Already have an account? Log in**. New week, choosing or swapping a dinner, meal details, the meal advisor, and checkout confirm without an account still open sign-up. Onboarding does not ask for an account. Site URL `https://getplentry.com`. Redirects `https://getplentry.com/**` and `https://plentry.vercel.app/**` (old host kept so mail already sent still opens).
- **Onboarding:** shop, **what you eat** (everything / vegetarian / vegan / pescatarian), optional dinner tags (meal prep is not offered unless already saved), household size and UK postcode, dinners/week **1–7 bar**, then a searchable cupboard list (oils, aromatics, spices). Goals and the budget slider are not steps. Diet and tags can be changed in **Profile**. Profile can set a weekly budget, and clearing that field turns it off. A new account has none until they set one.
- **Servings (LIVE 6 Oct 2026):** cooking for is **2, 4, or 6** (`prefs.servings`), defaulted from household size. The current week locks that number (`weekServings`) so a later Profile change applies next week. Week cards say **serves N** and add 5 minutes at 4, 10 at 6. Basket lines are whole packs (`2 × 500g`) from `meals.portions` (2-serving base). Seasonings stay 1 pack. **Garam masala** is one of those seasonings: one jar per week at any servings (2, 4, or 6), never scaled, the same as salt, pepper, paprika, cumin, chilli flakes and mixed herbs. Live 13 Oct 2026 (PR #10). Dinners with no portions add one extra pack of a shared ingredient only when the spare in the rounded pack is under half a pack (8 Oct 2026; several such dinners share that one extra). Recipe steps stay the 2-serving method; for 4 and 6 the modal shows the scaled grocery list and the “Written for 2” note. Shipped 6 Oct 2026 as Vercel `dpl_FYr7g7rS4RyU2jNEeUt3pzNkbFg1` with `pay` v29. Current production is the 23 Oct ship of PR #26 (see Last production ship). 
- **Week:** up to N dinners (`prefs.meals`, 1–7). The week is shorter when fewer published meals fit the diet. On 21 Oct 2026 the reviewed catalog is 52 of 77: 21 meat, 8 fish, 17 vegetarian, 6 vegan. Vegan New week stops at 6. Vegetarian can also use those vegan dinners (23). Pescatarian drops meat (31). Omnivore can use all 52. **Modify** (and New week) pick from **verified** meals in Postgres (`reviewed_at` set), never the AI. Unverified rows stay off New week / Modify. Modify lists 4–6 other verified dinners (6 when at least 6 fit the diet and are not already in the week), ranked with the same diet and tag weights as New week. **Show 4 more** skips meals already shown for that slot and anything already in the week. Fewer than 4 shows what is left. None left is a short message and a disabled refresh; **Start again** brings the first page back once the list has moved on. A vegan week that already holds all 6 vegan dinners shows that message and no empty grid. Picking one swaps that slot and rebuilds the basket and that dinner’s basket share. A dinner’s price is its penny share of this week’s whole-pack basket at 2, 4, or 6 servings. The cards sum to **Basket £**. A pack only one dinner uses is that dinner’s. A shared pack (oil, seasonings, a partly used pack) is split by ingredient-cost weight: scaled portion ÷ pack × that shop’s first priced row. Seasonings are not inside the weight. A dinner with no usable ingredient cost counts as one average-cost dinner on a shared pack. Leftover pennies use largest remainder, and a tie goes to the earlier day. Modify simulates the swap and shows that share. A dinner that owns no basket line, because every ingredient it uses is ticked in the cupboard, shows **From your cupboard** on the day card and on a Modify option. A share that rounds to 0p while the dinner still owns a line shows the price, including £0.00. A missing shop price on a loaded shelf shows **Price soon** to a customer and **incomplete** to an admin. No shop, or a shelf that has not loaded, uses the catalogue price the way the basket does. The footer is **Basket £** + delivery + 5%. **Beef & Tomato Tortilla Casserole** has no portions, so its ingredient-cost weight is incomplete; the day card still shows its basket share when the lines have prices. `supabase/meal_ingredient_prices.sql` stores the ingredient-cost weight and is not applied to production. The week does not grow. Week length stays N when the published catalog has enough meals that fit, and is shorter when it does not. Catalog dinners have **tags**; New week / Modify / auto-pick **weight** meals whose tags match `prefs.tags` if set, otherwise saved onboarding goals (`decide` / `shop` / `waste` → quick, meal_prep) **and diet**. A new account has no goals, so that map adds nothing. Saved goals and a saved budget stay on the profile blob and are not cleared. Default diet is **omnivore** (meat/fish first, at most one vegetarian/vegan dinner on New week) until onboarding/Profile → What you eat is vegetarian / vegan / pescatarian. Those three diets filter New week and Modify (vegetarian keeps vegetarian and vegan, vegan keeps vegan, pescatarian drops meat). Omnivore does not hide meals. Tag weighting does not hide the rest of the live catalog. The week screen compares the shop with a saved budget. A new account has no budget, so New week does not apply the ×1.2 first-pass cap and the week screen has no under/over line. A stored number, including a previously saved £60, still does both. Profile can set one. `pay` does not read goals or budget. The day-card shares are the basket split described above (not an even split and not a quantity split). Under the cards: Basket £ + delivery + 5% = the total, and a line that the card hold adds a 30% buffer and only the actual amount is captured. **This shop** is that total to the penny. Live 23 Oct 2026 (PR #26, merge `7f1453ea`, static `dpl_BARhEjagaW5Lbn4f4y22CheQ85Kh`). The 15 Oct ship (`dpl_ByazVmgr1eDffUFhqEwwWRDdTVea`) was the earlier quantity split.
