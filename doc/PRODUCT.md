# Product

## Goal

Get people using Plentry **instead of HelloFresh**: a week of dinners they chose, groceries from a UK supermarket they already know, delivered to their door. Plentry plans, prices, and places the shop. The supermarket supplies the food.

Job to be done: *“Keep my kitchen stocked with dinners I will actually cook, at a fair supermarket price, without me thinking about it every night.”*

## Pitch

Tick what’s already in the cupboard (or skip). It builds a week of dinners, skips oils and spices you already have, prices the missing basket at Tesco, Sainsbury’s, Asda and Waitrose, then **we order it for you**. Card hold at checkout; you pay the **exact store total + 5% Plentry fee**. No HelloFresh box, no packing markup, no subscription (yet).

## Who

Busy UK households (pilot: London-ish postcodes, founder-led concierge). First paying users are friends and early adopters, not a mass-market app store launch.

## Money

- **Groceries:** store shelf / till price (we do not mark food up).
- **Plentry:** **5% of the store total** (including typical delivery fee as shown).
- **Hold:** `(groceries + 5%) × 1.30` so the till can come in high (raised from 1.15 on 19 Sep after Waitrose was ~15% above shelf).
- **Capture:** Ops types the real supermarket total; the `pay` function charges that **plus 5%**, never more than the hold.
- **Not yet:** live Pepesto quotes (optional), subscriptions, split-store baskets.

## What we are not

Not a warehouse. Not a recipe social network. Not “pay the supermarket inside their app” for the concierge launch — **the customer pays Plentry via Stripe**; Noe (Ops) shops at the store and marks the order through.
